About AgriSense

Farming decisions, backed by data

AgriSense brings together a crop and livestock encyclopedia, disease and pest identification, weather guidance, market prices and an AI farm assistant into one platform — built so that every page answers a real question a farmer is asking.

Our Mission

Give every farmer, regardless of farm size, access to the same quality of agronomic guidance that larger operations rely on.

What We Cover

26 crops, 8 livestock types, a disease and pest library, weather guidance, market prices, and an AI assistant available on every page.

How It's Built

A lightweight, fast, HTML/CSS/JavaScript platform — no heavy frameworks, so it stays usable even on slower connections and older devices.
